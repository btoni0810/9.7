Sub-BIN: cp2112_usb_scan-USB device scan for CP2112 SMBus/I2C Bridge
Lupin Debug Flow - usb_reset_test_group-cp2112_detected-FAIL-MEASUREMENT
Product: Lupin (LUPINE BF4)
Fail: First phase of usb_reset_test_group - USB scan expects VID 10c4 / PID ea90 (Silicon Labs CP2112)
Schematic: Page 110 - USB to I2C (CP2112); Page 106 - USB HUB
DUT device: U122 (CP2112-FB3-GMR)
Reset AND gate: U301 (SN74LVT08)
Notes before starting
- Bus / Device numbers in lsusb may vary by station. For this BIN, only ID 10c4:ea90 is required.
- Do not treat DNP / X / NA parts as populated (e.g. C8287, R3253).
- I2C data bytes (0x89, 0x09, 0x40, ...) are read-modify-write and can differ by expander state / station. Prefer the logical set_io(bank, pin, data) mapping; use the exact I2C sequence from a known-good log on the same station when possible.
- Open the mux path once before writing to expander 0x23:
i2c -a 0 -d 1 /dev/mst/mtusb-1 w 0x72 0x0 0x0
i2c -a 0 -d 1 /dev/mst/mtusb-1 w 0x77 0x0 0x2
U301 / CP2112 logic (quick reference)
- U301 = AND of S_MASTER_RST_A17_L and S_MASTER_RST_L -> S_AND_RST_L -> R3515 -> S_I2C_BRIDGE_RST_L -> U122 RST*
- Pin 4 HIGH (~3.3 V) -> CP2112 out of reset -> device present in lsusb
- Pin 4 LOW (~0 V) -> CP2112 held in reset -> device absent
- Test polarity:
- MASTER_RST_L low -> CP2112 disappears; high -> reappears
- MASTER_RST_A17 expander set_master_rst_17_high (data=1) -> CP2112 disappears; set_master_rst_17_low (data=0) -> reappears

Step 8 - Configure and validate U301 input / output states
====================================================================================================
Use /dev/mst/mtusb-1. Expanders: 0x23 (mux + MASTER_RST_L), 0x22 (MASTER_RST_A17).
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 8.1 - Open mux path and enable USB MUX
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
i2c -a 0 -d 1 /dev/mst/mtusb-1 w 0x72 0x0 0x0
i2c -a 0 -d 1 /dev/mst/mtusb-1 w 0x77 0x0 0x2
enable_usb_mux - set_io(0x1, 0x1, 0x0) on 0x23:
i2c -a 1 -d 1 /dev/mst/mtusb-1 r 0x23 0x1
i2c -a 1 -d 1 /dev/mst/mtusb-1 w 0x23 0x5 0x0
i2c -a 1 -d 1 /dev/mst/mtusb-1 r 0x23 0xd
i2c -a 1 -d 1 /dev/mst/mtusb-1 w 0x23 0xd 0xfd
Wait 1 second.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 8.2 - Set MASTER_RST_L high
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
set_master_rst_l_high - set_io(0x0, 0x7, 0x1) on 0x23:
i2c -a 1 -d 1 /dev/mst/mtusb-1 r 0x23 0x0
i2c -a 1 -d 1 /dev/mst/mtusb-1 w 0x23 0x4 0x89
i2c -a 1 -d 1 /dev/mst/mtusb-1 r 0x23 0xc
i2c -a 1 -d 1 /dev/mst/mtusb-1 w 0x23 0xc 0x7e
Wait 2 seconds.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 8.3 - Disable USB MUX
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
disable_usb_mux - set_io(0x1, 0x1, 0x1) on 0x23:
i2c -a 1 -d 1 /dev/mst/mtusb-1 r 0x23 0x1
i2c -a 1 -d 1 /dev/mst/mtusb-1 w 0x23 0x5 0x2
i2c -a 1 -d 1 /dev/mst/mtusb-1 r 0x23 0xd
i2c -a 1 -d 1 /dev/mst/mtusb-1 w 0x23 0xd 0xfd
Wait 1 second.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 8.4 - Set MASTER_RST_A17 high
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
set_master_rst_17_high - set_io(0x0, 0x6, 0x1) on 0x22:
i2c -a 1 -d 1 /dev/mst/mtusb-1 r 0x22 0x0
i2c -a 1 -d 1 /dev/mst/mtusb-1 w 0x22 0x4 0x40
i2c -a 1 -d 1 /dev/mst/mtusb-1 r 0x22 0xc
i2c -a 1 -d 1 /dev/mst/mtusb-1 w 0x22 0xc 0xbf

Step 8.5 - Validate U301 high / low vs applied states
